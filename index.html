Four Pavilions & The Centre Stage

Thematic spaces and main performance arena at Watamu Beach Grounds.

Taste of Watamu

Coastal cuisine, Swahili and Mijikenda flavors, wood-fired baking, and local food artisans.

Destination Watamu

Indigenous craft, heritage storytelling, traditional arts, and community enterprises.

International Hub

Cross-cultural exchange, Indian Ocean delegations, and global visitors.

Marine Hub

Ocean stewardship, reef protection, artisanal fishing heritage, and coastal conservation.

The Centre Stage

Main performance arena hosting live taarab, chakacha, bango, Mijikenda percussion and dance troupes, and evening showcases across all three days.

Ocean Competitions

Maritime traditions and shoreline sports on the waters of Watamu.

Dhow Racing

Traditional sailing dhows competing across Watamu's coastal waters.

Fishing Competition

Local fishermen and coastal anglers taking part in a regulated sea competition.

Swimming Competition

Open-water coastal swimming races along a marked shoreline course.
