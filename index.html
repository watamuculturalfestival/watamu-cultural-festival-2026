Discover the Soul of Watamu

Destination Watamu: Sun, Sand & Soul

Watamu is more than a destination; it is a vibrant tapestry of Swahili culture, marine life, and community. Our festival brings this to life across four key pillars—Heritage, Arts, Flavors, and Ocean—creating an unforgettable three-day experience on the Kenyan coast.
